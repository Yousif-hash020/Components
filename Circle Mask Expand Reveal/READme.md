Award-winning websites (jaise **Awwwards**, **CSS Design Awards**, **FWA**) par typically ye common "signature" effects milte hain:

**Scroll-based effects**
- **Scroll-triggered zoom/circle reveal** (jo abhi banaya) — sections cover/reveal hote hain
- **Horizontal scroll sections** — vertical scroll ko horizontal movement mein convert karna
- **Scroll-driven text animation** — words letter-by-letter fade/slide in hote hain scroll ke sath
- **Parallax layers** — different speed par background/foreground move karte hain
- **Sticky/pinned storytelling sections** — jaisa humne banaya (stacking, zoom, circle)
- **Scroll progress indicators** — thin bar ya circular progress top par

**Cursor/mouse effects**
- **Custom cursor** (magnetic buttons — cursor button ki taraf khichta hai)
- **Cursor trail/particles**
- **Image distortion on hover** (liquid/glitch/RGB split effect jab image ke upar cursor jaye)
- **Magnetic hover** — buttons/links thora sa mouse ki taraf move karte hain

**Page transitions**
- **Smooth page transitions** — click karne par naya page slide/fade/wipe se aata hai (normal reload nahi lagta)
- **Preloader animations** — site load hote waqt ek creative loading animation

**Text & typography**
- **Text scramble/glitch effect** — letters random change ho ke asal text ban jate hain
- **Split text reveal** — har letter/word neeche se slide up hota hai
- **Marquee/infinite scrolling text**

**3D & WebGL**
- **3D model interaction** (Three.js) — product ko drag/rotate kar sakte ho
- **WebGL shader backgrounds** — liquid, noise, gradient distortion animations
- **Tilt effect on cards** — mouse move karne par card 3D tilt karta hai (perspective)

**Micro-interactions**
- **Hover image reveal cards** — hover karne par image/color change
- **Button fill/morph animations**
- **SVG line-drawing animations**

Inme se koi bhi effect banwana ho to naam bata dena (jaise "magnetic button", "text scramble", "horizontal scroll section", "tilt card") — wahi tarz mein bana dunga.
